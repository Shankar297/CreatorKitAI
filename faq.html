Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CreatorKit AI

General

What is CreatorKit AI? ▾
CreatorKit AI is a free, all-in-one content creation toolkit available on the Google Play Store. It provides 16+ creative tools including AI-powered caption, hashtag, hook, post, and video script generators, a stylish Unicode font converter with 100+ styles, a gaming nickname generator, an Instagram bio generator, a decorative symbol library, and a curated AI facts feed. It also features a Play section with 6 interactive games. CreatorKit AI is designed to help content creators, social media influencers, and gamers create engaging content faster while having fun.
Do I need to create an account to use the app? ▾
No! CreatorKit AI uses secure, anonymous device-based authentication. When you first open the app, a unique Device ID is generated automatically. This allows you to save favorites, track history, and manage your preferences without the hassle of signing up for an account. No personal information like your name, email, or phone number is required.
Which platforms does CreatorKit AI support? ▾
Our tools are optimized for all major social media platforms including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Threads, Facebook, and WhatsApp. You can generate platform-specific content tailored to each platform's best practices and audience expectations.

Features

What is the Stylish Font Converter? ▾
The Font Converter is CreatorKit AI's core feature. It transforms your regular text into 100+ unique Unicode font styles that work across all platforms — Instagram bios, WhatsApp statuses, gaming profiles, Facebook posts, and more. You can preview, copy, share, and favorite your converted text. Some premium font styles are available exclusively for Premium subscribers.
How does the AI Caption Generator work? ▾
Simply enter a topic and select a category (Travel, Motivation, Fitness, Technology, Food, Nature, Fashion, and more). Our AI engine, powered by Google Gemini and OpenAI, generates engaging, platform-ready captions for you. You can copy, share, or save them to your favorites for later use.
What is the AI Engagement Predictor? ▾
The Engagement Predictor analyzes your content before you post it. Enter your content type, topic, hook, caption, hashtags, and target audience, and our AI evaluates your content's structure to provide a virality score with color-coded ratings (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, or Poor) along with actionable recommendations to maximize your reach and audience retention.
What is the AI Video Script Generator? ▾
The Script Generator creates ready-to-film scripts for short-form video content. Choose your platform (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels), duration (15s to 90s), style (Educational, Storytelling, Motivational, Funny, and more), tone, and language. Each script includes an attention-grabbing hook and structured body content.
What is the AI Facts Feed? ▾
The AI Facts Feed is a curated, infinite-scrolling feed of fascinating facts about AI, technology, robotics, and more. Facts are sourced from reputable sources including Wikipedia, academic journals, and official tech publications. You can browse by category, listen to facts with text-to-speech, bookmark your favorites, and share interesting facts with others.
What tools are included in Bio, Nickname & Symbols? ▾
This includes three tools: (1) Instagram Bio Generator — create professional bios with customizable categories, emoji preferences, and personalization; (2) Stylish Nickname Generator — generate decorated gaming nicknames with Unicode symbols for Free Fire, BGMI, Instagram, and more; (3) Symbol Library — browse and copy decorative symbols, stars, hearts, arrows, gaming symbols, and special characters.
What games can I play in the app? ▾
The app features a Play section with 6 interactive games: Jungle Maze, Kids Runner, Candy Match, Bow Master 3D, Carrom, and Ludo. You can earn coins or watch rewarded ads to unlock premium game shops and progression items while you take a break from creating content!

Premium

What can I do for free? ▾
Free users get access to basic font conversion, the full Symbol Library, Bio Generator, Nickname Generator, WhatsApp Text Generator, AI Facts Feed, and 3 daily AI generations per tool type. That's a lot of creative power at no cost!
What does Premium include? ▾
Premium unlocks the full CreatorKit AI experience: 100+ premium font styles, up to 20 daily AI generations per tool type (1000+ monthly), access to all AI-powered tools without restrictions, an ad-free experience, and smart clipboard detection that automatically converts copied text. Premium is available as Monthly, Yearly (best value), or Lifetime plans.
How do I subscribe to Premium? ▾
You can upgrade to Premium directly within the app. Tap on any premium feature or visit the upgrade screen to choose your plan. All payments are securely processed through the Google Play Store. Your subscription can be managed (upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled) through your Google Play Store account settings.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▾
To cancel your Premium subscription, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon, go to "Payments & subscriptions" → "Subscriptions," find CreatorKit AI, and tap "Cancel subscription." Your premium benefits will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Data & Privacy

Do I own the AI-generated content? ▾
Yes! Any caption, hashtag, hook, post, script, or bio generated by our AI tools based on your prompts is yours to use freely on your social media platforms, websites, or any other channels. There are no restrictions on how you use the generated content.
Is my data safe? ▾
Yes. CreatorKit AI uses industry-standard security measures including JWT authentication, HTTPS encryption, and rate limiting. We do not collect personal information like your name or email. Your AI prompts are sent to our AI providers (Google Gemini / OpenAI) solely for content generation and are not permanently stored. You can delete all your data at any time through the app's Settings.
How do I delete my data? ▾
Go to Profile/Settings in the app and use the "Delete All User Data" option in the Danger Zone section. This will permanently remove all your stored data including favorites, history, and device registration from our servers.

Support

Is the AI-generated content always accurate? ▾
While we use top-tier AI models (Google Gemini and OpenAI) to generate high-quality content, AI can occasionally produce inaccurate or unexpected results. We recommend reviewing all AI-generated content before publishing. For AI Facts, source links are provided so you can verify the information independently.
